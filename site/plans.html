Billing rules (approved Nov 9)

Show the 7 rules
  • Plan credits are added every month and don't roll over to the next month.
  • Yearly plans are paid upfront at about 20% off; credits are still added monthly.
  • Upgrades and downgrades start at your next billing date.
  • If you cancel, your plan runs to the end of the period you paid for.
  • Extra credits you buy stay separate and don't expire at renewal. Plan credits are used first.
  • No surprise charges: when credits run out, nothing is billed automatically.
  • Your current balance and free previews stay as they are.

What credits buy today

  • Preview: 12–15 credits (3 lifetime free previews per account)
  • HD export: 38–52 credits, depending on video length (up to 30 s)
  • If a render fails, its credits are returned automatically.
