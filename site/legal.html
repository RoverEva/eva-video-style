Terms, privacy and refunds

The service

EVA Video Studio edits a talking-head video you upload (up to 30 seconds and 200 MB) into a short video with captions, motion graphics and optional music. Each account gets 3 free watermarked previews (up to 10 seconds each). After that, previews and full 1080p exports are paid with credits bought through Stripe: US$9.99 buys 100 credits; a preview costs 12–15 credits and an HD export 38–52 credits, depending on the video's length. The exact cost is shown before anything is used. There is no subscription.

Your content

Only upload videos you own or have permission to use. You keep all rights to your video. We use it only to produce your edit. You must not upload unlawful content or content that infringes anyone's rights.

Music

Background music is original music composed for EVA Video Studio. You may use it in the video you bought, including commercially, on any platform.

Storage and deletion

Files are kept in private cloud storage and are only reachable through short-lived private links. Your videos are saved privately in My Videos. Original uploads and previews are deleted automatically after 30 days, HD exports after 90 days, and you can delete any of them earlier yourself. Account and credit records (email, balance, payment references) are kept while your account exists, for support and accounting. Payment card details are handled by Stripe and never reach us.

Refunds

If a preview or HD export fails, nothing is used: free previews remain available and credits are returned to your balance automatically. Exporting the same version again, or downloading it again, is free while it is kept. For anything else, contact us within 14 days of purchase.
