EVA Video Studio

Terms, privacy and refunds

The service

EVA Video Studio edits a talking-head video you upload (up to 30 seconds and 200 MB) into a short video with captions, motion graphics and optional music. A watermarked preview of up to 10 seconds is free. The full 1080p video costs US$9.99 per video, paid through Stripe. There is no subscription.

Your content

Only upload videos you own or have permission to use. You keep all rights to your video. We use it only to produce your edit. You must not upload unlawful content or content that infringes anyone's rights.

Music

Background music is original music composed for EVA Video Studio. You may use it in the video you bought, including commercially, on any platform.

Storage and deletion

Files are kept in private cloud storage and are only reachable through short-lived private links. Original uploads and previews are deleted automatically after 3 days. Paid HD videos are deleted automatically after 7 days. Order records (order id, payment reference, email) are kept for 90 days for support and accounting. Payment card details are handled by Stripe and never reach us.

Refunds

If your paid video can't be produced, we retry automatically and, if it still fails, refund the full amount automatically. If you are charged twice for the same video, the extra charge is refunded automatically. For anything else, contact us within 14 days of purchase.
