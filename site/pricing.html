Questions

Is this a subscription?

No. $9.99 is a one-time purchase of 100 credits. Buy another pack whenever you need more; nothing renews.

How are credits used?

Your first 3 previews are free. After that a preview costs 12–15 credits and a full HD export 38–52, depending on the video's length (up to 30 seconds). The exact cost is shown before you start.

What do I get with an HD export?

The full-length video in 1080p (1080×1920 or 1920×1080), no watermark, rendered from your original upload with the look of the preview you chose. Download it again any time while it's kept.

What if a render fails?

It doesn't count: a free preview stays free and credits go straight back to your balance.

How long are my videos kept?

Previews and your original upload for 30 days, HD exports for 90 days. Each video shows its expiry date, and you can delete it any time.

Can I use the music commercially?

Yes. Every track is an original composition made for EVA Video Studio and is cleared for use in your video.

Refunds

For anything other than a failed render, contact us within 14 days of purchase. See terms, privacy and refunds.
