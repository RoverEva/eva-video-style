# Zodiac · Glow Beat / 12 星座光之卡点

12 videos, one per zodiac sign. Each runs 29.4 seconds at 16:9 and uses the same music and beat logic as the MBTI template (`template/mbti`). The timeline, the camera and the beat hits are the same. The skin changes per sign: the element palette (fire orange, earth olive, air silver, water deep blue), the hero's look and emblem (horns, mane, moon, scorpion tail, circling fish, twins), the sign prop (sword, orb, lantern, star staff, scales, bow, water urn, wheat), the constellation drawn into the sky at the title, the sign glyph beside the title, and four trait tags.

12 支星座短片，和 MBTI 模板同一首音乐、同一套卡点。每个星座换元素配色、人物造型和标志物、手持道具、星座连线和符号，以及四个特质词。

```bash
python3 template/zodiac/cards.py out/zodiac_cards                                          # 12 hero cards + overview
python3 template/zodiac/render.py --sign leo --music media/MBTI_music.mp3 --stills 2.8,8.4,24.4 --out out/check
python3 template/zodiac/render.py --sign all --music media/MBTI_music.mp3 --out out/zodiac/       # all 12 videos
```

- Data per sign (look, emblem, prop, constellation, element) is in `zodiac_signs.py`. The script per sign is in `signs/<key>.json`: `captions` holds the 10 caption slots (line 1, line 2, gold word), `traits` the four trait tags, and `variants` 4 MBTI-style letters that pick the section visuals (E streaks / I calm rings, F orb / T diagram, J flags / P sky lanterns).
- The music track and logo follow the MBTI template: put the track in `media/` (not committed) and use `template/mbti/brand.json` for the logo.
